\section{Summary and Discussion}
\label{ch:summary-and-discussion}

Summary in your words, not just of the paper, but of your work processing the paper and related works. Your own observations and conclusions as well. Did you like the paper? Why? Why not? What you expected to encounter. What you did? What were the main ideas of the paper? Why it is a contribution? What is the contribution, etc. Strengths, weakness. Your analysis. If you use someone else's material, cite it properly.
